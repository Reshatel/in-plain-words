Privacy policy

In plain words

  • I only collect what you type into the form: name, phone, email, topic and your story.
  • I use it only to reply to you and prepare for the consultation.
  • Nothing is sold or shared — except with a court or notary when your case needs it, and only with your consent.
  • The price calculator keeps your answers in your own browser, not on a server.
  • Write to hello@inplainwords.law and I will delete everything within 7 days.

1. Data controller

In Plain Words law office (“the Office”), 14 Paper Lane, Suite 3, Springfield, acts as the controller of personal data submitted through this website. The Office is fictional and this website was created as a test assignment.

2. Categories of data processed

The Office processes identification and contact data (name, telephone number, email address), the subject matter of the enquiry, preferred consultation date and format, any free-text description voluntarily provided by the data subject, and, where attached, a non-binding cost estimate generated by the data subject.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed solely for the purpose of responding to the enquiry, scheduling a consultation and taking steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract for legal services, on the basis of consent and pre-contractual necessity.

4. Retention

Enquiries that do not result in an engagement are erased no later than 12 months after the last communication. Data relating to engagements is retained for the period required by professional regulations governing attorneys.

5. Disclosure

Personal data is not sold, rented or disclosed to third parties, save to courts, notaries, translators or other professionals where strictly necessary for the provision of legal services and with the prior consent of the client, or where disclosure is required by law.

6. Browser storage

The website stores the state of the cost calculator, the attached estimate and a summary of the submitted request in the local storage of the data subject’s browser. No analytics or advertising cookies are used. This data never leaves the device and can be erased by clearing site data.

7. Rights of the data subject

The data subject has the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of personal data, to object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Contact

Requests concerning personal data may be sent to hello@inplainwords.law or by post to the address above. The Office responds within 30 days.
